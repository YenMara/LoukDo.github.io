About LoukDo

Connecting Cambodia's talented artisans with

Our Mission

To empower Cambodian artisans and local sellers by providing them with a platform to showcase their craftsmanship and reach a wider audience. We believe in preserving traditional skills while supporting sustainable livelihoods.

Our Vision

To become Cambodia's leading marketplace for authentic local products, where every purchase supports community development and cultural preservation. We envision a thriving ecosystem of artisans and conscious consumers.

Our Story

LoukDo was founded in 2026 with a simple yet powerful idea: to create a bridge between Cambodia's rich artisan heritage and modern consumers who appreciate quality and authenticity. Our journey began when we witnessed firsthand the challenges faced by talented craftspeople in reaching customers beyond their local communities. Traditional markets were limited by geography, and many beautiful handmade products never found their way to appreciative buyers. Today, LocalMarket has grown into a vibrant community of sellers and customers, all united by a shared appreciation for authentic Cambodian craftsmanship. Every product sold on our platform represents not just a transaction, but a story of tradition, skill, and dedication.
